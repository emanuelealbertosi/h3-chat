H3-Chat audio remix uses a private standalone audio.cpp CLI, commit 13c4192a28d6a212f075c4cbefc5e4983e6ed52a (Apache-2.0), with the H3 artifact patch. Original sources: https://github.com/0xShug0/audio.cpp/tree/13c4192a28d6a212f075c4cbefc5e4983e6ed52a . Rebuild: scripts/build-remix-runtime.ps1. Vocal alignment/conversion helpers adapted from the user-owned H3-Music project. No runtime dependency on H3-Music. FFmpeg 7.1.1 full GPL-3.0-or-later build from https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/ ; source and build dependencies: https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg and https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.1.tar.xz . Model weights are selected separately.
